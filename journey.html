2024 — Present
Triage Security Engineer 1
Arctic Wolf Networks

I investigate endpoint, network, and identity alerts (malicious URLs, account takeovers, breaches) over the phone and by ticket. When something is live, I contain the device and get it to Tier 3 fast enough to limit what an attacker can do.

One I'm proud of: a teammate had triaged an anomalous sign-in from a low-prevalence hosting provider as a medium. Looking at the activity around it, I saw RCL activity, MFA modifications, and anomalous token use. I raised it to high, submitted a callout, and contained the device. That's a bold call for a Tier 1, and when Tier 3 joined the ticket they first read it as a medium too. I held my ground, the activity kept coming, and we reached the customer. They wrote back confirming the user was compromised and thanking us for stopping it.

When I see the same gap causing repeat problems, I push for a real fix: an account-takeover alerting bug, a risky button on the triage dashboard, and a metric that discouraged teamwork (see Projects). I'm also the go-to person for over half of our Tier 1 analysts and interns, and I received Arctic Wolf's peer-nominated Golden Wolf award for leading our cultural events and lifting team morale.
